# STLD_ASG2_ShwunLeiWin



## 🎮 Game Overview

Welcome to Escape Beep Borp, an immersive sci-fi exploration game where you must scavenge for resources to repair your ship and unlock the path to the alien base.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Platforms & Hardware Requirements

* **Engine:** Unity 
* **Platform:** PC (Windows)
* **Hardware:**


* **Display Settings:** Recommended 1920x1080 resolution. Windowed or Fullscreen modes are both supported.

### Key Controls

* **Movement:** `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` keys.
* **Look:** Mouse movement.
* **Interact:** Approach alien NPCs or specific objects.
* **Menu:** `ESC` to open the Pause/Options menu.

## 🧩 Puzzle Walkthrough & Answer Key

To complete the mission, follow these steps:

1. **Bridge Repair:** Explore the starting area to find **3 Energy Crystals**. Once collected, return to the collapsed bridge area to trigger the bridge repair.
2. **Special Orb Placement:** Locate the **Special Orb** near your crashed rocket. Carry it to the end of the bridge to open up the green wall of goo.
3. **Metal Scraps:** Collect **3 Metal Scraps** scattered throughout the alien base area. Once all items are collected and the base is powered, you will have completed the mission.

## 🛠️ Known Limitations & Bugs

* **Bridge Trigger:** Ensure you do not leave the bridge area while the "collapse" event is playing, as the camera transition may be jarring.
* **Collision:** Some assets may have tight colliders; if you feel stuck, use the WASD keys to strafe out of geometry.
* **UI Refresh:** The HUD counter updates upon object collection; if it fails to update, ensure the `GameManager` object exists in the hierarchy.
